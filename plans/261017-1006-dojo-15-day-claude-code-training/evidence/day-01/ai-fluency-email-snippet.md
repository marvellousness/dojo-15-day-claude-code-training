# AI Fluency — short version for the daily report email

**AI Fluency: Framework and foundations** (Claude Academy, 14 lessons, ~4h): <status: completed / in progress — quiz score x/10>

Key learnings:
- **AI Fluency** = working with AI effectively, efficiently, ethically and safely, built on four competencies (the 4Ds).
- **Delegation:** decide what I do, what AI does and what we do together, based on the goal (Problem Awareness), what the tool can do (Platform Awareness) and how to split the work (Task Delegation).
- **Description:** tell AI *what* to produce (Product), *how* to approach it (Process) and *how to behave* (Performance). Six prompting techniques: context, examples, constraints, steps, think first, role/tone.
- **Discernment:** check the output (accuracy, relevance), the reasoning (logic gaps) and the behaviour, then loop: Describe, Discern, Refine, Integrate.
- **Diligence:** choose tools responsibly, be open about AI's role, and verify and own everything I share.
- Three modes of use: Automation (AI does a task), Augmentation (we think together), Agency (AI acts on my behalf).
